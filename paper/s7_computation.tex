\section{The computation}\label{sec:computation}

\subsection{The scan}
For each interval $[L_1, L_2]$ we search for kernel parameters $(c, f)$:
first over the grid $c\in\{40,50,\dots,100\}$, $f\in\{0.5,\dots,0.9\}$, then locally over $c\pm10$ in steps of $5$ and $f\pm0.1$ in steps of $0.05$.
Given $(c,f)$, we put $\tau_{12} = \eps_R(L_2+1)/(1 + 2/L_1)$ and choose $\eps$ by three fixed-point steps so that the smoothing term of Proposition~\ref{prop:master} is approximately $f\tau_{12}$.
The search is done in floating point; it only selects $(c, \eps)$.
We then evaluate the right-hand side of Proposition~\ref{prop:master} in ball arithmetic for the selected $(c,\eps)$, with the actual smoothing term (not $f\tau_{12}$), $N^+$ from \S\ref{sec:density}, $\lambda^*$ from Theorem~\ref{thm:H} and $H = 3\cdot10^{12}$ (\S\ref{sec:ball}).
The ratio $q$ is the upper end of this right-hand side divided by the lower end of $\tau_{12}$; an interval is certified when $q < 1$.

The scan starts with intervals of width $10$ and bisects any interval that is not certified, down to width $0.5$ (\code{certify/arb/scan\_arb.py}; the same cover is produced by the floating-point driver \code{code/interval\_adapt.py}).
For $[43, 57)$ we only need some bound, since this range enters only through the integral in Lemma~\ref{lem:transfer}; there we use widths $2$ to $0.5$, and $q$ may exceed $1$.

The binding range $L\approx 900$ corresponds to bandwidth $T_1 \approx e^{30}$.
There the zero sum is dominated by $\lambda = (1-\beta)\log T \in [0.20, 0.32]$ and $\log T\in(28.73, 30]$.
For the interval $[900,910]$ of width $10$, the best kernel gives $0.40$ (smoothing) $+ 0.60$ (zeros above $H_0$) $= 1.002$, plus negligible terms; intervals of width $5$ are certified.

\subsection{Certificates}
The following list gives, for each ingredient, the program that certifies it.
All these programs are in the repository; \code{code/rerun\_all.sh} re-runs the whole chain.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Lemma~\ref{lem:epsR} ($\eps_R$ on every cell): \code{certify/epsR\_cert.py}.
\item Lemma~\ref{lem:smooth} (smoothing constant): \code{certify/smoothing\_const.py}.
\item Proposition~\ref{prop:transfer} (from $\psi$ to $\pi - \li$, both conditions of \eqref{eq:target}): \code{certify/arb/transfer\_arb.py}.
  The worst ratio is $0.99382$, at $\log y = 877$.
\item Lemmas~\ref{lem:W0}--\ref{lem:L12} (constants of \cite{BTY}): in \code{rep/}, the programs \code{consts\_cert.py}, \code{lemma5\_cert.py}, \code{lemma11\_cert.py}, \code{f\_nonneg\_cert.py}, \code{mp\_cert.py} and \code{misc\_cert.py}.
\item Theorem~\ref{thm:H}: \code{rep/lamcert.py}, with output \code{rep/lamstar2c.json}.
\item Theorem~\ref{thm:rep}: \code{rep/repcert\_run.py} and \code{rep/min\_fix.py}; output \code{rep/dstar2c\_all.json}.
\item Theorem~\ref{thm:G}: in \code{lemG/}, the programs \code{box2.py} and \code{kcert2.py}; the constants of the contour remainder and of $S_t$ in \code{certify/arb/remconst.py}.
\item Theorem~\ref{thm:density}: the parameters are stored in the tables \code{lemmaD\_tableGH0.01\_*.pkl} (produced by \code{bt4.py}); every cell's bound is re-evaluated from its parameters by \code{certify/arb/cells\_arb.py}, and the two Gamma integrals $I_\Gamma \le 5.922$ and $\le 4.72$ of Lemma~\ref{lem:D} are certified by \code{certify/arb/gammaint\_cert.py}.
\item The scan: \code{certify/arb/scan\_arb.py}, output \code{certify/arb/scan\_arb\_\{0,1\}.txt}; \code{certify/coverage\_check.py} checks that the certified intervals cover $[57,3159)$.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Ball arithmetic}\label{sec:ball}
Items 1--9 are certified in ball arithmetic.
Floating-point arithmetic enters only through discrete or continuous \emph{choices}: the kernel parameters $(c,\eps)$, the table parameters $(u,\tau,w,x)$, the auxiliary parameters $\eta$, $\kappa$ and $a$ in Lemmas~\ref{lem:pair}--\ref{lem:crowd} and Theorem~\ref{thm:G}, and the partition points of the Stieltjes sums.
Each of these is fixed as an exact binary number before the bound is evaluated, and the bound holds for every admissible choice.
In detail:
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Density cells} (\code{cells\_arb.py}). All $3320$ non-empty cells are re-evaluated from their stored parameters.
  The value $-\zeta'/\zeta(1+\eta)$ is computed exactly from a power series of $\zeta$, so the cruder bound $1/\eta - 0.36$ is not used.
  The fractional knapsack in Lemma~\ref{lem:pair} is bounded by weak duality, $\lambda M + \sum_i\max(0, b_i - \lambda c_i)$ for any $\lambda\ge0$, instead of by the greedy value.
  Window counts are floors of certified upper bounds.
  The window sum in $I'$ receives an explicit tail for $y > 60$.
  The certified values exceed the floating-point table values by at most $1.3\cdot10^{-11}$ in $\log N^+$.
\item \emph{Lemma G constants} (\code{remconst.py}). The sup of $|\mathcal A|$ over the $3$-torus for $p \le 100$ is evaluated on the same grid and with the same cell enclosure as before.
  This uses interval arithmetic on doubles with outward rounding after every operation; the IEEE operations $+,-,\times,/,\sqrt{\ }$ are correctly rounded, and the values of $\cos$ and $\sin$ at the grid angles come from \code{arb}.
  All other constants (Gamma integrals, Gaussian integrals, $\int|\Gamma|(\mathcal K_c + E)$, $Q_0$, values of $\zeta$) are upper Riemann sums of monotone integrands or closed forms evaluated in \code{arb}.
\item \emph{Gamma integrals} (\code{gammaint\_cert.py}). On $|t| \le 60$, $\Rp\psi(\sigma+it) > 0$ is certified on $[-\frac12,-0.44]\times[0,60]$ by adaptive boxes, so $|\Gamma(\sigma+it)|$ increases with $\sigma$ and the supremum over $\sigma$ is attained at the right end.
  The integral there is computed with \code{acb.integral}, and $|t| > 60$ is bounded explicitly.
  This gives $I_\Gamma \le 5.92164$ and $4.71363$ for the $\mathcal J$-integral.
\item \emph{Scan} (\code{scan\_arb.py}). For every interval of the cover, $\eps_R(L_2+1)$ comes from the expansion of Lemma~\ref{lem:epsR} (lower end), and the smoothing term uses the actual $\kappa(\eps)$.
  The Stieltjes sum carries the factor $e^{\eps(c+1)/6}$ of Lemma~\ref{lem:stieltjes}, and its last partition point is rounded up past $\log T_1$.
  A block is dropped only when $s \ge 1 - \lambda^*(\log t_{i+1})/\log t_{i+1}$ holds in ball arithmetic.
  The cell indices of Theorem~\ref{thm:density} are computed in exact rational arithmetic, and $s_{\max}$ is rounded up.
\item \emph{Transfer} (\code{transfer\_arb.py}). This repeats \code{transfer\_check.py} in \code{arb}, with $\eps_R$ enclosed on each cell of width $1/20$ by Lemma~\ref{lem:epsR}.
\end{itemize}
On the binding range $[857,917)$ the ball-arithmetic values of $q$ agree with the floating-point ones to about $10^{-9}$.
At $L = 43$ the ball value is larger by $0.05$; this is the fixed-point residual in the smoothing term, which is now evaluated exactly rather than set to $f\tau_{12}$.
The whole chain takes about $1.5$ hours on two cores (\code{certify/arb/run\_all.sh}).

\subsection{Adversarial audits}
Two adversarial audits were carried out by separate instances of the same AI system that assisted with the computations (see the acknowledgements); they are not independent in the human sense.
The first audit covered the new ingredients (\S\S\ref{sec:zfr}--\ref{sec:density}) and checked the following points, confirming them:
\begin{itemize}
\item the legitimacy of keeping the second pair in Lemma~6 of \cite{BTY};
\item the per-$\sigma$ form of Lemma~5;
\item the induction in Theorem~\ref{thm:H};
\item the use of $d^*$ in the pair sum;
\item the error constants;
\item the replacement of $\mathcal J$.
\end{itemize}
It found one genuine defect: the clamp on $\nu$ described at the end of \S\ref{sec:rep}.
It also found three minor gaps: a missing non-negativity certificate for the dropped Lemma-10 terms, the unrounded $w$-derivative values, and the range of the Hiary--Patel--Yang bound.
All four were fixed, and the full chain was re-run with identical results.

A second audit, of a draft of this paper, found an unjustified rescaling: the density constants, which are valid at the corner $\delta_k$ of a cell, had been applied with the actual $\delta$ inside the cell.
This was removed (Theorem~\ref{thm:density} now uses the corner value), and the scan was re-run.
That audit also corrected the definition of $N_R$, the attributions for the lower range, the hypotheses of Lemmas~\ref{lem:L5} and~\ref{lem:epsR}, the statement of the correction factor $\iota$, and the reproducibility scripts.
